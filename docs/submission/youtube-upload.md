# Upload the demo to your YouTube account

The prepared recording is for your account. Uploading and public submission are your remaining external actions.

1. Sign in to [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/) with the account you want to use. Choose **Create → Upload videos** and select the prepared video. WebM is one of YouTube's [supported upload formats](https://support.google.com/youtube/troubleshooter/2888402?hl=en).
2. Write your own title and description. Identify that the demonstration uses a fictional deterministic sample; add your public repository link. Do not add claims about live-model performance that the footage does not demonstrate.
3. Choose audience and other declarations accurately for your video. Follow YouTube's current prompts; do not accept unrelated defaults without checking them.
4. Set visibility to **Public**, as the hackathon rules require a public YouTube or Vimeo video. Wait for processing and publication.
5. Copy the watch URL. Open it in a signed-out/private browser window and check that the whole video plays, is under three minutes, and needs no permission or payment.
6. Put that URL into the current Devpost submission form and record it in `rules-checklist.md`. Keep it accessible through judging.

The agent has not uploaded or submitted anything on your behalf. Send the public video URL back if you want it checked against the submission requirements. Current upload mechanics are documented in [YouTube's official guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407?hl=en).

After upload, open [the hackathon's My projects page](https://devpost.com/submit-to/30447-build-with-ai-basics/manage/submissions). The signed-out page shows registration; the exact submission fields and exit-survey prompts require your account. Follow the actual form, write your own answers, and review rule acceptance yourself. A repository push and a prepared local video are not a submitted entry.
